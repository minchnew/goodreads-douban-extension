# Chrome Web Store Listing Draft

## English

**Name**

Goodreads + Douban

**Summary**

Independent, unofficial extension showing Douban ratings and popular short comments on Goodreads book pages.

**Description**

See Douban book information while browsing Goodreads. On Goodreads book detail pages, Goodreads + Douban searches Douban Books using the book title and shows the matching book's author, overall rating, star rating, rating count, and up to five popular short comments.

Comments include available author names, star ratings, dates, locations, and helpful-vote counts. The panel is draggable and links to the matching Douban book page.

This is an independent, unofficial extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or produced by Goodreads or Douban. The Goodreads and Douban names are used only to identify compatible services; their names and marks belong to their respective owners.

For the lookup, the extension sends the Goodreads book title directly to Douban. The browser may include your existing Douban cookies in requests so Douban can recognize your session. The extension does not read or store cookie values and does not send lookup data to an extension developer server. See the [Privacy Disclosure](https://github.com/minchnew/goodreads-douban-extension/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## 简体中文

**名称**

Goodreads + Douban

**摘要**

独立、非官方扩展：在 Goodreads 图书页面显示豆瓣评分和热门短评。

**说明**

浏览 Goodreads 图书详情页时查看豆瓣图书信息。Goodreads + Douban 使用图书标题搜索豆瓣读书，并显示匹配图书的作者、综合评分、星级、评分人数，以及最多五条热门短评。

短评会显示可用的作者名称、星级、日期、地点和有用票数。面板可以拖动，并提供匹配豆瓣图书页面的链接。

本扩展由独立开发者制作，非 Goodreads 或豆瓣官方产品，与两家公司无关联，也未获其认可、赞助或背书。Goodreads 和豆瓣名称及标志仅用于说明兼容服务，相关权利归各自所有者所有。

为完成查询，扩展会将 Goodreads 图书标题直接发送给豆瓣。浏览器可能会在豆瓣请求中附带你现有的豆瓣 Cookie，以便豆瓣识别你的登录状态。扩展不会读取或保存 Cookie 值，也不会将查询数据发送到开发者运营的服务器。详见[隐私披露](https://github.com/minchnew/goodreads-douban-extension/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)。
